How to read this

Starchy

Dry and fluffy when cooked. They fall apart in water but give crisp fries and light baked potatoes.

All-purpose

Medium starch. Creamy enough to mash and firm enough to roast. The safe choice when you are unsure.

Waxy

Low starch, high moisture. They keep their shape when boiled, which suits salads and gratins.
